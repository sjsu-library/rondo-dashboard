Make your own copy of the data template

A Google Sheet template just opened in a new tab. This sheet is where all of your exhibit's content lives — its pages, items, and captions. You'll make your own copy of it, then paste that copy's link below.

  1. Make your own copy. In the template tab (sign in to Google if prompted), open File → Make a copy. In the dialog, give it a name and click Make a copy. Google opens your new copy in a new tab.
  2. Publish your copy to the web. In your new copy, open File → Share → Publish to web, then click Publish and confirm. This is required without it, your exhibit's GitHub Pages site can't read the Sheet once the repo is created.
  3. Copy your copy's link. Switch to that new tab and copy the full web address from the browser's address bar. It looks like: docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1AbC…long-id…xyz/edit
  4. Paste it below and click Confirm.

⚠ Paste the link to your copy — not the template you opened from. Your copy has a different ID in its address.
